Senior Software Engineer, Backend Platform
Northwind Cloud | Remote (US) | Full-time

Who we are
Northwind Cloud builds developer infrastructure trusted by thousands of engineering teams. Our mission is to make shipping reliable software boring. We're a remote-first company of 300 people, backed by top-tier investors.

The role
As a Senior Software Engineer on the Backend Platform team, you'll design and build the distributed systems that power our API, handling billions of requests per day. You'll work with a small, senior team and have real ownership over architecture decisions.

Responsibilities
• Design, build and operate scalable backend services in Go and Python
• Own the reliability of core APIs: define SLOs, improve observability, and participate in on-call
• Lead technical design reviews and write clear design docs
• Improve performance and reduce latency across our request path
• Build and maintain infrastructure with Kubernetes and Terraform on AWS
• Mentor other engineers and raise the bar on code quality and testing

Requirements
• 6+ years of professional software engineering experience
• Strong proficiency in Go, Python, or Java
• Deep experience with distributed systems and microservices
• Experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL and caching with Redis
• Hands-on experience with Kubernetes, Docker and CI/CD pipelines
• Experience with AWS or GCP
• BS in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience

Bonus points
• Experience with Kafka or other message queues
• Contributions to open source projects
• Experience with gRPC and Protocol Buffers
