Data Analyst, Marketplace Analytics
Cartwheel
Chicago, IL · On-site · Full-time

About us
Cartwheel is the largest online marketplace for used bikes and outdoor gear in North America. Since 2015 we've connected millions of buyers and sellers. We're a profitable, fast-growing company headquartered in Chicago.

Position summary
Cartwheel is seeking a Data Analyst to join our Marketplace Analytics team. You will turn data into insights that help us balance supply and demand, improve conversion, and grow seller retention.

Key responsibilities
1. Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau that track marketplace health metrics
2. Write complex SQL queries to analyze buyer and seller behavior
3. Partner with product and marketing teams to define KPIs and measure the impact of launches
4. Analyze A/B test results and communicate recommendations to stakeholders
5. Conduct deep-dive analyses on conversion funnels, cohorts and retention
6. Automate recurring reporting using Python

Basic qualifications
1. 2+ years of experience in data analysis or business intelligence
2. Advanced SQL skills
3. Experience with a BI tool such as Tableau, Looker or Power BI
4. Proficiency in Python or R for data analysis (pandas, NumPy)
5. Solid understanding of statistics, including hypothesis testing and regression
6. Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, Computer Science or a related quantitative field

Preferred qualifications
1. Experience in e-commerce or two-sided marketplace companies
2. Familiarity with dbt and modern data warehouses like Snowflake or BigQuery
3. Experience with experimentation platforms

Pay range: $85,000 - $105,000

Competitive salary, annual bonus, 401(k) match, health, dental and vision insurance, gear discounts, and a dog-friendly office.
