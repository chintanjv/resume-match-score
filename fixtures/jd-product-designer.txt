Product Designer
Bloomhaus — San Francisco, CA (Hybrid, 3 days in office)

About Bloomhaus
Bloomhaus is a consumer wellness app helping 4 million people build healthier routines. We believe great design is the core of everything we do. Our values: care deeply, ship thoughtfully, and stay curious.

About the role
We're hiring a Product Designer to join our Growth & Onboarding squad. You'll own end-to-end design for experiences that help new members discover value in their first week, working closely with product, engineering and research.

What you'll do
- Own the end-to-end design process from discovery to launch for onboarding and activation flows
- Create user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes in Figma
- Plan and run usability testing and user interviews, and synthesize insights into design decisions
- Contribute to and help evolve our design system
- Partner with product managers and engineers to define problems and ship iteratively
- Use data and A/B test results to inform design decisions and measure impact
- Present your work and rationale in design critiques

Who you are
- 3+ years of experience as a product designer, ideally on consumer mobile apps
- A portfolio that shows strong interaction design, visual design and product thinking
- Expert in Figma, including prototyping and component libraries
- Experience conducting user research and usability testing
- Comfortable working with design systems and accessibility standards (WCAG)
- Clear communicator who can explain design decisions to stakeholders

Nice to have
- Experience designing for iOS and Android
- Familiarity with motion design or prototyping in Framer or ProtoPie
- Experience in health or wellness products

- Wellness stipend and free Bloomhaus Premium
- Flexible PTO
